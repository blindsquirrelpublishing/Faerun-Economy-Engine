## What It Is

The engine's `bottle` trade unit weighs 3 lb. This page reads that as about 1.3 lb of glass holding 0.2 gallon (a little under a quart). Bottles are sold with the drink, so unlike kegs and casks they are a material, not equipment. A stoneware bottle is the cheap alternative.

**At glass make cost:** 1.3 lb at 0.133 gp a lb ([glassware formulation](../../commodities/product/glassware.md#formulation-proposed), 2.66 gp a crate) plus the cork is about **0.18 gp**. The drinks' multi-level roll-ups use this figure.
