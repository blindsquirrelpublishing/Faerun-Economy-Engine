**Used in:** [Brandy](../../commodities/drink/brandy.md), ops 10 and 20

## What It Is

A copper pot with a domed head and a coiled copper worm running through a tub of cold water. Wine boils in the pot, the vapour condenses in the worm. Brandy usually goes through twice: a stripping run, then a spirit run where the distiller cuts off the first and last of the flow.
