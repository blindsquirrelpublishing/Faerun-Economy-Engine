## What It Is

A masonry chamber with a perforated drying floor (tiles or haircloth on battens) over a firebox. Germinated grain is spread on the floor and dried for about a day to stop it growing. Smoke from the fire flavours the malt, which is one reason a smoky ale like [Elminster's Choice](../../commodities/drink/elminsters-choice.md) is plausible.

## Capacity and Life

- **Capacity:** One day's germinated grain per firing
- **Service life:** Life of the building; drying floor relaid every few years
