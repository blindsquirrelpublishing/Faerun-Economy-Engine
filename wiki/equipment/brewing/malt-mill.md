## What It Is

Crushes kilned malt coarsely so the mash can reach the starch without turning to paste. A small brewer uses a hand quern; most send malt to the local grain mill, which takes a share of the grain as its toll.

- **Capacity:** A hand quern grinds a few bushels a day, enough for one brewhouse
- **Service life:** 10 years; stones redressed
