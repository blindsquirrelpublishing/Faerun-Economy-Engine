# Glass furnace

[Equipment index](../README.md) · [Glassmaking](README.md)

**Status:** Proposed; not in the engine  
**Group:** Glassmaking  
**Kind:** Structure, fixed  
**Used in:** [Glassware](../../commodities/product/glassware.md), ops 20 and 30

## What It Is

A domed brick furnace with ports for the crucibles and a fire chamber beneath; the fritting chamber sits beside it. Burns day and night once lit.

## Capacity and Life

- **Capacity:** Four crucibles, about 25 lb of glass a day
- **Service life:** Relined yearly; rebuilt every 5 to 10 years
