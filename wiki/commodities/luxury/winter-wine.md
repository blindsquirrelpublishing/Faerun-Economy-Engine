# Winter wine

**Status:** Proposed; not in the engine

**Closest engine commodity:** [Fine vintage wine](../luxury/wine-fine.md)

**Source:** *Aurora's Whole Realms Catalogue* (TSR 9358, 1992, AD&D 2nd edition)

## Catalogue Description

Strong, sweet ice wine from grapes frozen on the vine, tinted blue by Chessentan berries; served as a dessert wine.

**Origin given:** The North.
