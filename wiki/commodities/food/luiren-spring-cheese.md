**Source:** *Aurora's Whole Realms Catalogue* (TSR 9358, 1992, AD&D 2nd edition)

## Catalogue Description

Soft, pungent halfling cheese that intoxicates and is habit-forming for halflings. Restricted: sold only at the Waterdeep outlet, and only with written permission from a city authority or temple.

**Origin given:** Luiren.
