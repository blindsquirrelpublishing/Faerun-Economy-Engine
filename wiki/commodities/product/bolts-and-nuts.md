# Bolts and nuts

[Commodity index](../README.md) · [Manufactured products](README.md)

**Status:** Proposed; not in the engine  
**Suggested engine category:** product  
**Closest engine commodity:** [Nails and fittings](../product/nails.md)

## Catalogue Description

Eight-inch threaded bolts in four diameters, cut to size, with matching nuts.
