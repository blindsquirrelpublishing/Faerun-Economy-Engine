## Overview

Orlumbor is a small, rocky and barren island city-state in the Sea of Swords. Its
people are goat-herders, fishers and some of the most renowned shipwrights in
Faerun, and it builds and repairs many of the ocean-going ships of the North. The
town is cut into the island's rock around a natural harbour facing the mainland.
Despite its size it stays independent, helped by the demand for its shipwrights,
by Waterdeep's patronage and protection and, from 1364 DR, by a close alliance with
Baldur's Gate. [Source][frwiki], citing the *Forgotten Realms Campaign Set*
(1987), p. 68; *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* (2nd edition, 1993), p. 68;
*A Grand Tour of the Realms*, p. 112; *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, p. 8;
*Passage to Dawn*, chapter 4.

## Geography

- Due west of the Trollbark Forest, between Daggerford and Baldur's Gate, and
  about 300 miles south of Waterdeep on the Sword Coast. Few people visit, partly
  because the harbour is treacherous (*A Reader's Guide to R. A. Salvatore's the
  Legend of Drizzt*, pp. 140 to 141). The [Trollbark Forest][trollbark] article
  also places the island west of the forest, with Dragonspear Castle to the
  north-east.
- The populated area is the mainland-facing natural harbour. Buildings are
  cave-like structures and complexes cut into the rock, like the shipwrights'
  homes, linked by tunnels and flights of stairs; the docks always seem busy
  (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 2nd edition, p. 68; *A Grand Tour of the
  Realms*, p. 112).
- The island was wooded long before the 14th century DR but had been completely
  logged by 1367 DR (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 2nd edition, p. 68).
  The bronze dragon Lhammaruntosz laired on mountains in the island's
  "bowl-valley" in the 14th century DR (*Dragons of Faerun*, p. 43).
- Nearby: Farr Windward, a cliff-top village of exiles west or south-west of the
  town, reached by a long, monster-haunted tunnel trail (the Tunnels of Orlumbor);
  a hidden shrine of Talos south of the city; a cave holding a magical gate to a
  wooded lot in Neverwinter (*Treasures of the Savage Frontier*, 1358 DR, video
  game; *Secrets of the Magister*, p. 127). [Delmarin Island][delmarin], home of
  fog giants, lies west of Orlumbor and north of Mintarn.
- Engine: one neighbour, a 263.2-mile sea lane to Baldur's Gate (3.7 days, "Sea of
  Swords coasting run"). There is no lane to Waterdeep (about 268.6 miles by
  straight line on the engine map), Daggerford (about 172 miles) or Mintarn. See
  OR11 and OR12.

## Government

- 1358 DR: Tulgar Wrighttson, the island's leader, who after a false-flag attack by
  Luskan pirates and a protest from Farr Windward freed that colony (*Treasures of
  the Savage Frontier*, video game only).
- No other ruler or form of government is recorded. Orlumbor's independence rests
  on Waterdeep's patronage and protection (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 2nd
  edition, p. 68), it is closely allied with Baldur's Gate by 1364 DR (*Passage to
  Dawn*, chapter 4), and it is listed as a Lords' Alliance member in [Lords'
  Alliance][alliance], citing *Storm King's Thunder* (2016), p. 80 (circa 1491 DR).
- Conflicts: wars with Mintarn before the Tyrant took power there (survived with
  Waterdeep's aid); later conflicts with Baldur's Gate and Amn; Luskan's High
  Captains launched a war of conquest against Orlumbor that failed (*Forgotten
  Realms Campaign Setting*, 2nd edition, p. 68; [Mintarn][mintarn], citing *A Grand
  Tour of the Realms*, p. 111; [High Captains of Luskan][hcl]).
- Religion: temples of Gond and Umberlee and a hidden shrine of Talos (*Treasures
  of the Savage Frontier*; *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, p. 50). Stormhaven House,
  the Umberlee temple, is led by a High Trident and was one of the leading temples
  of the faith after 1358 DR (*Faiths & Avatars*, 1996, p. 174, via the wiki's
  [Umberlee][umberlee] and [Church of Umberlee][chumb] articles).

### Families and organizations

Local families owning all docks and warehouses, with Waterdhavian noble families
holding interests ("So Saith Ed"); Stormhaven House, temple of Umberlee, which
traditionally sends representatives to lead Waterdeep's Fleetswake (*City of
Splendors: Waterdeep*, p. 50); temple of Gond; a Kraken Society, Host Tower and
Zhentarim alliance using the hidden Talos shrine in 1358 DR (*Treasures of the
Savage Frontier*); the Company of the Brazen Pennant ferry (1358 DR, video games);
the exile colony of Farr Windward, freed in 1358 DR.

resident a year), exports 461.2 gp a day, imports 138,316 gp a day. The new `herd`
1 makes sheep (81.7 head a day exported against 42 a tenday in stock), raw wool
(2.27 bales a day of surplus) and more fresh meat (44.1 sides a day exported); it
does not make goats, which are not an engine commodity.

## Lore Follow-ups

1. **1492 DR state.** No source read describes Orlumbor after the 14th century DR
   beyond the Lords' Alliance listing (*Storm King's Thunder*, p. 80) and the
   shipwright reputation (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, pp. 8, 70). Check those
   pages for a ruler, population and the shipyards' state (OR6, OR7).
2. **Population.** Check the 1st-edition *Forgotten Realms Campaign Set*, p. 68,
   and the 2nd-edition *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, p. 68, for a figure.
3. **Stormhaven House link.** The Orlumbor article links "Stormhaven House" to a
   page about a Talos temple of that name in Urmlaspyr, Sembia (*Forgotten Realms
   Adventures*, p. 114); the Umberlee temple on Orlumbor (*Faiths & Avatars*, p.
   174; *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, p. 50) has no page of its own.
4. **Maps.** No 2nd-edition map of the island was found; identify the publication
   of File:Baldur's Gate Area.jpg and check the 1993 campaign setting maps (OR12).
5. **Amn and Waterdhavian interests.** The "So Saith Ed" post on private docks is
   undated; check which Waterdhavian noble families hold interests.
6. **Farr Windward.** Freed and made a free community of Orlumbor in 1358 DR
   (video game only); whether it still exists is unknown.
7. **Mintarn.** The Orlumbor and Mintarn wars ended when the Tyrant of Mintarn
   took power; check against the Mintarn record when Mintarn is reviewed.
