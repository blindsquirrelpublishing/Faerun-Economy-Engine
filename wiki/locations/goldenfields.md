# Goldenfields

[Location index](../README.md)

**Region:** Dessarin Valley, on raised ground on the western side of the River Dessarin, south of Bargewright Inn (engine region `Dessarin Valley`, zone `dessarin`)  
**Settlement type:** Walled abbey-farm of Chauntea; small town  
**Reference period:** 1355 to 1372 DR lore (2nd- and 3rd-edition sources), with a circa 1485 to 1491 DR snapshot (5th edition); 1492 DR planning baseline  
**Evidence reviewed:** 2026-10-05  
**Engine ID:** `goldenfields`

This entry paraphrases the [Forgotten Realms Wiki article][frwiki] (a secondary
source) and the wiki articles it links. Its history and early size come from
*Volo's Guide to the North* (1993) and the *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*
(3rd edition, 2001); its crops, livestock, buildings and people come mostly
from *Storm King's Thunder* (2016), which describes the abbey in the late
1480s or early 1490s DR. That is the closest source to the 1492 DR baseline,
but nothing here is verified for 1492 DR itself.

## Overview

Goldenfields, called the Granary of the North, is a walled temple-farm of
Chauntea and the largest provider of grain, fruit and vegetables in the
North. Inside its walls are a small town of several dozen longhouses, an
abbey, an inn, a brewery, and the farms, orchards and gardens it is known
for. It was founded around 1355 DR by Tolgar Anuvien, a Chauntean cleric and
former member of the Crazed Venturers of Waterdeep. [Source][frwiki], citing
*Volo's Guide to the North*, p. 43, and *Storm King's Thunder*, pp. 44 and 48.

## Geography

- Built on raised land within the Dessarin Valley (*Storm King's Thunder*,
  p. 44), on the western side of the River Dessarin, south of Bargewright
  Inn, at the end of the Northfurrow Trail (*Volo's Guide to the North*,
  p. 33; *Storm King's Thunder*, p. 45).
- Size grew from about 20 square miles (*Volo's Guide to the North*, p. 43)
  to nearly 30 square miles by 1370 DR (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*,
  p. 176). *Storm King's Thunder* says the farmland covers "more than" 20
  square miles; the wiki's note treats the figures as consistent.
- Enclosed by a mortared stone wall about 60 feet high on the outside,
  20 feet on the raised inside and about 30 feet thick, with stone towers
  and barracks every 1 to 2 miles along it (*Storm King's Thunder*, p. 44).
- **Region classification:** the infobox gives the broad "Sword Coast North".
  *Storm King's Thunder* places it in the Dessarin Valley, and the wiki files
  its people under "Inhabitants of the Dessarin Valley". The engine's
  `Dessarin Valley` is kept, as settled for Amphail (A6).
- Engine: `river` is false and the only neighbour is an 8.2-mile wilderness
  track to Goldenfields Northeast Junction (see G7; routes are a later phase).

## Economy and Trade

| Direction | Goods (wiki infobox, citing *Storm King's Thunder*, p. 48, and *Volo's Guide to the North*, p. 44) |
| --- | --- |
| Imports | None listed |
| Exports | Food: grain, rice, wheat, herbs, oranges, apples, corn, barley, hops, pumpkins, onions, squash, tomatoes, potatoes, radishes |

- Crops (*Storm King's Thunder*, p. 48): rice, maize, barley and hops among
  the grains; apple and orange orchards; herb and vegetable gardens with
  carrots, onions, potatoes, tomatoes and squash. The wiki's Rhubarb article
  adds rhubarb in the gardens.
- Livestock (*Storm King's Thunder*, p. 46): paddocks where oxen and cattle
  graze; smaller pens of sheep, pigs, turkeys and chickens.
- Goldengulp Brewery: about 60 workers brewing the namesake beer, served at
  Northfurrow's End (*Storm King's Thunder*, pp. 45, 47, 48).
- Earth Mother's Bounty sells seeds and ornamental cornucopias to visitors
  (*Storm King's Thunder*, pp. 45, 46).
- Goldenfields supplies much of the food eaten in Waterdeep and nearby
  settlements (*Volo's Guide to the North*, p. 44; *Storm King's Thunder*,
  p. 44), and gives it without seeking payment, in Chauntea's honour
  (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, p. 176). See G10.
- The wiki's Cordial article says the monks of Goldenfields made medicines,
  wines and cordials, but its citation is broken (no title or URL), so this
  is unverified.

## Government and Defense

- Founded and first run by Tolgar Anuvien (ruler in 1366 DR per the
  infobox). Ellardin Darovik, a Tethyrian priest of Chauntea, is abbot in
  1491 DR (*Princes of the Apocalypse*, p. 33; *Storm King's Thunder*,
  p. 47). Who leads in 1492 DR is unverified.
- In the 14th or 15th century DR the Emerald Enclave gained influence; its
  members are as welcome as Chauntean clergy (*Storm King's Thunder*, p. 44).
- Defence: Chauntean priests patrol the lands (*Volo's Guide to the North*,
  p. 43); guardsmen, hired adventurers and Emerald Enclave treants protect
  the complex (*Storm King's Thunder*, p. 44). The castellan is appointed by
  the Lords of Waterdeep; in the late 1480s or early 1490s DR the post was
  ceremonial (Hantanus Tarm, *Storm King's Thunder*, p. 46).
