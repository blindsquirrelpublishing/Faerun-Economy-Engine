## Overview

Griffon's Nest is a permanent settlement of the Uthgardt Griffon tribe. By
1485 DR it had declined to a hamlet of approximately 300 people under Chief
Halric Bonesnapper. The source describes poverty and reduced openness to
outsiders, unlike the prosperous community of earlier periods. [Source][frwiki]

## Geography

The settlement lies in the Surbrin Hills northeast of Longsaddle, with Shining
Creek farther east. This supports a hills setting, not a measured water supply,
river port, route length, or relocation of the map marker. [Source][frwiki]

## Economy

The article lists steel weapons as imports and woven rush goods, cane baskets,
and trunks as exports. Earlier descriptions include roughly a dozen outlying
farms. These trade and farming details belong to a multi-period account, not
a verified inventory of businesses or production in 1485 or 1492 DR.

Kralgar encouraged outside trade; the later account under Halric describes a
poorer, more isolated community. No trade volumes, prices, crop yields, or
current carrier services are supplied. [Source][frwiki]

## Government and People

Government is tribal, led by a chief. Kralgar Bonesnapper is recorded as chief
in 1358, 1366, and 1372 DR. Halric Bonesnapper, his great-grandson, is named in
1485 DR and is used for the latest dated reference here.

The article also names Chief Targ Keifer circa 1484 DR and his son Keyl. It
explicitly leaves the proximity of the Targ and Halric dates unresolved; no
succession sequence is inferred. Adalfus Stormgatherer is named as a shaman
under Kralgar, not as an attested resident in the latest period. [Source][frwiki]

## Historical Infrastructure

The mid-14th-century description records approximately twenty thatched
wood-and-earth huts, a central longhouse, two warehouses, a log palisade, and
outlying hillside farms. Those counts must not be reused as a surveyed layout
for the later hamlet. The source establishes no well yields, sewer system,
current building count, or street plan. [Source][frwiki]

## Chronology

- **1348 DR:** Kralgar becomes chief.
- **1358 and 1366 DR:** The article records 900 inhabitants and outside trade.
- **1372 DR:** The settlement reaches a reported population of 6,713.
- **After Kralgar:** The community declines over subsequent decades.
- **Circa 1484 DR:** Targ Keifer is separately named as chief.
- **1485 DR:** Halric presides over a hamlet of approximately 300 inhabitants.

Source: [wiki history and notes][frwiki]. Earlier details remain historical
annotations rather than being combined into a single contemporary profile.
