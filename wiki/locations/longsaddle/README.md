## Overview

Longsaddle is a small, sleepy hamlet on the Long Road in the Savage Frontier. It
is best known as the home of the Harpells, a large and eccentric family of
wizards whose spells are powerful but prone to backfire, and who live in the Ivy
Mansion on a hill above the village. Because of the Harpells the hamlet carries
more weight than its size suggests, and it is a member of the Lords' Alliance.
Ranchers work the grassland around it, and its recorded exports are beef and
mutton. [Source][frwiki], citing *The Savage Frontier*, p. 30; *The North*, p. 11;
*Storm King's Thunder*, p. 97.

## Geography

- East of the Crags and west of the Evermoors, on an isolated stretch of the Long
  Road (*The Forgotten Realms Atlas*, p. 79; *Storm King's Thunder*, p. 74).
- A broad grassland surrounds the village and supports the ranchers (*The North*,
  p. 11).
- The Hill Stream runs through the western side of the village and Saddle Pond
  lies in the north part of town (*Volo's Guide to the North*, p. 57).
- The Ivy Mansion stands on a hill overlooking the village, separated from it by a
  small stream; its compound has a stable, a farm building and the living quarters
  (*The Forgotten Realms Atlas*, p. 79, via the [Ivy Mansion][ivy] article).
- Mog's Keep, base of the ogre mercenary company Blue Sigil, lies nearby (*Gold &
  Glory*, p. 18). The Harpell werewolves known as the Bidderdoos lived in the
  forest around the village by 1479 DR (*The Companions*, p. 313).
- Engine: two road neighbours, Triboar (137.8 miles, 6.8 days, "Triboar to
  Longsaddle Road") and Mirabar (160.7 miles, 7.9 days, "Longsaddle to Mirabar
  Road"), both along the Long Road. Straight-line distance from Waterdeep on the
  engine map is about 270 miles. See LS14.

## Government

- A council of elders, in practice led by the Harpells (infobox, citing *Volo's
  Guide to the North*, p. 53, and *The Savage Frontier*, p. 30).
- Rulers recorded: Adanac Harpell, elder and spokesman around 1357 DR (*Waterdeep
  and the North*, p. 7); Ardanac Harpell, chief elder and spokesman, 1358 and 1370
  DR (*The Savage Frontier*, p. 30; *Volo's Guide to the North*, p. 53); Dowell
  Harpell, the family's spokesman and Longsaddle's representative on the Lords'
  Alliance council, 1489 DR (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, p. 7).
- Lords' Alliance member thanks to the Harpells (*Storm King's Thunder*, p. 97,
  circa 1491 DR).
- Religion: in 1376 DR two rival sects of Malar tried to found churches by a
  magically made lake and fought each other; the Harpells turned the most
  dangerous members into rabbits (*The Pirate King*, chapter 8). No temple is
  recorded later.

### Families and organizations

The Harpell family (Ivy Mansion; Lords' Alliance; allied with Mithral Hall;
enemies of the Talonmists of Westbridge); in 1368 DR the hamlet's families
included Beliver, Cadrasz, Dostril, Emmert, Feldryn, Gosstal, Harpell, Irimarl,
Kromlor, Mammlar, Ostever, Sharnshield, Silverhand-Arunsun-Maerdrym, Stormrider,
Suldivver and Zelorrgosz (*Volo's Guide to the North*, p. 52), with the noble Del
Goldwhites (*Dungeon Hack*; *Volo's Guide to the North*, pp. 57 to 59);
Perfectone Mercantile (1360s to 1370 DR); the Blue Sigil, about 450 ogre
mercenaries at nearby Mog's Keep under Mog the Fist around 1358 DR (*Gold &
Glory*, p. 18); the Bidderdoos werewolves (1479 DR); two Malarite sects (1376
DR).

## Lore Follow-ups

1. **1492 DR state.** The latest sourced facts are Dowell Harpell's spokesmanship
   (1489 DR) and the Lords' Alliance membership (*Storm King's Thunder*, p. 97).
   Check *Storm King's Thunder*, pp. 74, 97, for the village's condition, the
   Gilded Horseshoe and any population (LS3, LS4, LS9).
2. **Population scope.** Read *The Savage Frontier*, p. 30, and *Volo's Guide to
   the North*, p. 52, to pair the 130 and 1,100 figures with their years and
   scopes; the wiki notes the article still needs material from *The North*.
3. **Maps.** Inspect the 1996 *The North* village map and the 1999 Interactive
   Atlas plan to confirm the layout table and north orientation.
4. **Bidderdoos and Malarites.** Whether the werewolf Bidderdoos (1479 DR) and the
   Malarite churches (1376 DR) still exist near 1492 DR is unknown.
5. **Honor Among Thieves material.** The film and tie-ins are undated on the wiki;
   Bristle and Brettons, Marlamin and Gwinn may postdate 1492 DR.
6. **Westbridge feud.** The Harpell and Talonmist feud links to Westbridge, which
   is missing from the engine (see Locations Missing from the Engine).
